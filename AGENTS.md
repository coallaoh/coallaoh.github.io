# Publication updates

- Always move a newly accepted paper, or a batch of newly accepted papers, to the top of `data/publications.js`. Do not leave the entries in their old preprint positions. The renderer sorts by year and preserves array order within each year.
- Give every paper a short, friendly paragraph in `abstract`. Explain the problem, the contribution and why it matters in plain English. Avoid unexplained acronyms, copied technical abstracts and descriptions that only refer to another entry.
- Thumbnail options are a cropped, impressive figure from the paper, a symbolic object related to its theme, or a random image or emoji. Do not force the thumbnail to explain the paper. Check it at its rendered card size.
- Keep paper titles, author order and publication details faithful to the source. Do not invent findings to fill a missing description.
